# Lesson 07 — را / رو

**Goal:** Mark direct objects the way Persian does. There is no English equivalent for this, so it will feel arbitrary for about a week and then click.

---

1. Count aloud 20 → 1.
2. Write in script: *I have four books. How many chairs do you have?*
3. Read: ۱۷ · ۱۲ · ۶ · ۱۵

## 1. Grammar (16 min)

### 1a. The one rule

**را goes after a direct object when that object is definite** — when both speaker and listener know *which one*.

> من کتاب **را** می‌خوانم. — *I'm reading **the** book.*
> من کتاب می‌خوانم. — *I read books. / I'm reading a book.*

Same sentence, one word apart. را is Persian's way of saying "the specific one we both have in mind."

### 1b. Where it goes

**After the entire object phrase** — not after the noun, after the whole thing, ezâfe chain and all.

> من **ماشینِ جدیدِ نگار** را می‌بینم.
> *I see Negar's new car.*

را sits at the very end of the noun phrase, right before the rest of the sentence. Word order:

> **Subject — Object + را — (place/time) — Verb**

> من کتاب را به نگار می‌دهم. — *I give the book to Negar.*

### 1c. What's automatically definite

Some things are definite by nature and **always** take را:

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Proper names | نگار **را** می‌بینم — *I see Negar* |
| Pronouns | او **را** می‌شناسم — *I know him* |
| این / آن + noun | این کتاب **را** می‌خواهم — *I want this book* |
| Possessed nouns | کتابِ من **را** بخوان — *read my book* |

If you can put "the," "this," or a name in the English, you need را.

### 1d. Pronoun + را contracts

| Full | Contracted | Colloquial |
|---|---|---|
| من را | مرا (marâ) | منو (mano) |
| تو را | تو را | تورو (toro) |
| او را | او را | اونو (uno) |
| ما را | ما را | مارو (mâro) |
| شما را | شما را | شمارو (shomâro) |
| آنها را | آنها را | اونا رو (unâro) |

**مرا** is a formal/literary contraction you'll meet in writing and song lyrics constantly.

### 1e. When NOT to use را

This is where learners overcorrect. **Indefinite and generic objects take no را.**

> ✅ من کتاب می‌خوانم. — *I read books.* (generic)
> ✅ یک کتاب می‌خوانم. — *I'm reading a book.* (indefinite)
> ❌ یک کتاب را می‌خوانم. *(wrong unless you mean one specific book you've already mentioned)*

And **را only marks direct objects.** Not subjects, not indirect objects, not objects of prepositions:

> ❌ من به نگار را می‌دهم.
> ✅ من کتاب را به نگار می‌دهم. — *I give the book to Negar.* (کتاب is direct; نگار takes به)

### 1f. Why this matters more than it looks

Persian word order is fairly free, so را is often the **only** signal of who did what to whom:

> نگار مریم را دید. — *Negar saw Maryam.*
> نگار را مریم دید. — *Maryam saw Negar.*

Same three words. را carries the entire meaning. This is why native speakers never drop it, and why omitting it doesn't sound like a small accent — it sounds like a different sentence.

## 2. Vocabulary — transitive verbs (7 min)

These verbs *take* objects, so they're the natural home for را.

| Infinitive | Stem | Meaning | 1sg |
|---|---|---|---|
| خریدن (kharidan) | **خر** khar‑ | to buy | می‌خرم |
| فروختن (forukhtan) | **فروش** forush‑ | to sell | می‌فروشم |
| دادن (dâdan) | **ده** deh‑ | to give | می‌دهم |
| گرفتن (gereftan) | **گیر** gir‑ | to take, get | می‌گیرم |
| آوردن (âvardan) | **آور** âvar‑ | to bring | می‌آورم |
| بردن (bordan) | **بر** bar‑ | to take away, carry | می‌برم |
| بستن (bastan) | **بند** band‑ | to close | می‌بندم |
| شناختن (shenâkhtan) | **شناس** shenâs‑ | to know *(a person)* | می‌شناسم |
| باز کردن | *(compound)* | to open | باز می‌کنم |
| دوست داشتن | *(compound)* | to like, to love | دوست دارم |

**Two lexical notes:**

- **دانستن vs شناختن.** دانستن = to know a *fact*. شناختن = to know a *person or place* — to be acquainted. نگار را می‌شناسم = *I know Negar*; جوابش را می‌دانم = *I know the answer*. English collapses these; Persian doesn't.
- **دوست داشتن** is built on داشتن, so it takes **no می‌**: دوست دارم, not می‌دوست دارم.

> **من تو را دوست دارم** — man to râ dust dâram — *I love you.*
> Colloquially: **دوستت دارم** — dusset dâram. That's what you'd actually say to Negar.

## 3. Guided practice (10 min)

**Drill A — را or no را?** Decide for each, and say why.
1. I'm reading a book.
2. I'm reading the book.
3. I see Negar.
4. I buy bread. *(generic)*
5. I want this car.
6. I know her.
7. We drink tea. *(generic)*
8. She's selling her old car.

**Drill B — Translate into script.**
1. I see the book.
2. I know Negar.
3. She buys the new car.
4. We give the book to the teacher.
5. I don't know him.
6. They close the door.
7. I love you. *(informal)*
8. Negar brings the tea.

**Drill C — Placement.** Insert را in the correct position:
1. من ماشینِ قدیمیِ پدرم می‌فروشم.
2. او این کتابِ جالب می‌خواند.
3. ما آن خانه‌ی بزرگ می‌بینیم.

**Drill D — Fix the error.**
1. من یک کتاب را می‌خوانم.
2. من می‌بینم نگار را.
3. من به نگار را کتاب می‌دهم.
4. من نگار می‌شناسم.

**Drill E — Meaning flip.** Translate both, and explain the difference:
> نگار مریم را می‌بیند. / نگار را مریم می‌بیند.

## 5. How Negar Actually Says It

**را is never pronounced را in conversation.** It has two spoken forms:

| After a… | Becomes | Example |
|---|---|---|
| consonant | **‑و** (‑o), glued on | کتاب را → **کتابو** (ketâbo) |
| vowel | **رو** (ro), separate | این را → **اینو** (ino) · آنها را → **اونا رو** (unâro) |

| Formal | Colloquial |
|---|---|
| کتاب را می‌خوانم | کتابو می‌خونم |
| او را می‌بینم | اونو می‌بینم |
| من را | منو |
| این را بده | اینو بده |
| در را ببند | درو ببند |
| تو را دوست دارم | دوستت دارم |

**اینو** and **اونو** are among the most frequent words in spoken Persian. Get comfortable with them immediately — they're how you point at anything.

## 6. Negar Drill 🗣️

**Time ask: 6 minutes.**

> **Part 1 — Hearing the ‑و.** Say these six pairs and have Matt tell you which one has the object marker. It's a single vowel and Americans miss it entirely at first:
> ۱. کتاب می‌خونم / کتابو می‌خونم
> ۲. چای می‌خورم / چاییو می‌خورم
> ۳. ماشین می‌فروشم / ماشینو می‌فروشم
>
> **Part 2 — Point and command.** Point at things around the room and say **اینو ببین** / **اونو بده** / **درو ببند**. Have him repeat, then have him give *you* three instructions using اینو or اونو.
>
> **Part 3 — Correct him.** Have him say five sentences with objects. **Only correct را/رو** — ignore everything else. If he leaves it out, say the sentence back correctly and make him repeat.

## 7. Homework (20 min)

**H1 — Sorting drill (6 min).** Write 12 sentences, six with را and six without, alternating. Beside each, write one word explaining the choice: *definite* or *generic*.

**H2 — Verb cards (4 min).** Add today's ten verbs to your deck: infinitive / stem / 1sg.

**H3 — Rewrite (5 min).** Take your L04 daily-routine paragraph. Add objects to every sentence that can take one, with correct را. Then convert the whole thing to colloquial (رو / ‑و forms).

**H4 — Listening (5 min).** Find any Iranian song or short video clip with Persian subtitles. Write down five instances of رو or ‑و you hear. Note what each one is marking.

## Tutor notes

- Expect the student to either omit را constantly or slap it on everything. Both are normal. Overuse is the easier error to fix — correct undermarking first.
- Drill E is the lesson's payoff. If the student sees why word order can't be trusted without را, the rest follows.
- Placement after a full ezâfe chain (Drill C) is the mechanical skill that survives into Unit 2. Don't skip it.
- Watch for را after prepositional objects (D3) — a persistent overcorrection.
- دوستت دارم is a gift item. Give it, let them use it, don't analyze the ‑ت until L12.
